## Features

1.  ### Periodic
	- #### Every 10 Score - Ball Speed increase by 30
	- #### Every 20 Score Paddle side reduces by 10px  
2. ### Milestone
	- #### Score Of 30 - Spikes on ground (10 Sec)
	- #### Score of 50 - Control Reversal (20 Sec)
	- #### Score of 70 - Spotlight mode (10 seconds)
