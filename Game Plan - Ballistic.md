## Scoring Criteria 

| Scorer  | Ball Hit | Ball Miss | Opponent Miss |
| ------- | -------- | --------- | ------------- |
| Player  | +2       | -3        | +5            |
| Tracker | +1       | -2        | +5            |

## Features

``` 
Change in Plans: The game will punish you for losing - now you will score each time u hit the ball with paddle but the following chaotic punishments will be given whenever the opponent gets the respective score. 
```

1.  ### Periodic
	- #### Every 10 Score - Ball Speed increase by 30
	- #### Every 20 Score Paddle side reduces by 10px  <br> <br>
2. ### Milestone
	- #### Score Of 30 - Spikes on ground (10 Sec)
	- #### Score of 50 - Control Reversal (20 Sec)
	- #### Score of 70 - Spotlight mode (10 seconds)
